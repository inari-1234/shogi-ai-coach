## Pre-Reference corpus acceptance checklist (proposal, NOT frozen sampling rules)

1. **Rights and acquisition:** identify a named source/owner and record a dated policy URL or explicit written permission that covers automated/batch acquisition (if planned), storage, research/analysis, transformations, publication or nonpublication of raw and derived KIF, retention and opt-out. A user-supplied game from a third-party online platform still requires platform-specific terms review.
2. **Provenance and privacy:** keep only required metadata (game pseudonym/hash, relative skill stratum, time-control bucket, standard/variant, completion mode, source label, provenance receipt). Avoid raw handles, names, IPs, chats and location in CI/report artifacts. Where linkable accounts are unavoidable for audit, retain a private mapping separated from public manifests and determine deletion-request handling before acquisition.
3. **Eligibility before scoring:** standard 9x9 shogi only, legally parsable complete games, predeclared game-length/clock and result requirements, completed resignation or legitimate time result only if permitted by the eligibility plan; exclude explicit bot accounts, tagged engine games, known disqualifications, or obvious disconnect/abort artifacts through **pre-Reference** observable metadata. Ultra-short time limits and insufficient move-length are excluded only by thresholds frozen **before** any new game labels, not invented after inspecting BLUNDER occurrence. Any residual engine assistance is a documented sensitivity/contamination risk, not proof that every unflagged game is human-only.
4. **Precommitted skill strata:** use per-source rating quantiles or policy-defined numeric buckets, with **source-specific Glicko context**; do not equate Lishogi ratings numerically to 81Dojo/Wars or human dan/rank without calibration. Freeze boundaries, target game counts, source proportions and deterministic within-stratum sampling **before** Reference results. No after-the-fact BLUNDER/UNIQUE selection or moving games between tuning, untouched holdout and reserve.
5. **No selection by outcomes:** the original production Swift selector decides eligible positions. Do not use Reference loss, H5 eligibility, historical C1 errors, apparent candidate structure or user's rating as a *post-result* reason to replace a game or position. If A-only supply is insufficient, C (A-primary, preregistered B auxiliary) requires a separate Authority and separately reported human/engine outcomes; B-only cannot validate target users.
6. **Statistical gate feasibility:** H5 later requires at least 10 eligible Reference BLUNDER observations. The prior engine pilot yielded 0/16, which is not a population rate guarantee; the new source's H5 opportunity availability is **UNKNOWN until preregistered new-pilot measurement**. Preserve 10% Reference unresolved ceiling, 70% coverage floor, H1–H5 meanings, and distinguish source-validity issues from §12 operational budget changes.
7. **Contamination/protected state:** keep all historical five pilot game hashes, previously exposed pool games and B6 SHA exclusions, original manifests, current candidate head and raw evidence as immutable historical references. Any new source corpus version must bind a new deterministic key/seed, source-receipt digests, partition manifest SHA, source allocation and explicit game-level separation. Do not run a new pilot until the successor corpus Authority and source-policy permission are approved.
8. **Cost:** if 16M/64M Reference is selected in a **new** successor design, fix number of positions per shard, six root/target searches per position, <6-hour hosted job bound and complete raw SHA fail-closed merge before execution; shard status must never cause outcome-based sample replacement.
